## Headline result: LuSE ARDL Analysis

Over 2000 to 2025, GDP growth lifts the LASI (long-run coefficient 0.233, p = 0.044), inflation pulls it down (-0.092, p = 0.017) and a weaker Kwacha goes with a higher index (1.777, p < 0.001). About 28% of any deviation from the long-run path closes each year. [Read the full analysis](luse-ardl-analysis/).

## Headline result: Environmental Sustainability in Africa

Across 54 countries from 2000 to 2024:

- **Income still raises emissions.** The emissions turning point is about 64,000 PPP $ per person, beyond Africa's richest economy.
- **Growth goes with a falling renewable share of electricity** within countries.
- **Agricultural expansion goes with tree cover loss.**
- **Land-use change, not fossil fuel, is the larger CO2 source** in 25 of 53 countries.

[Read the full analysis](africa-environmental-sustainability/).
